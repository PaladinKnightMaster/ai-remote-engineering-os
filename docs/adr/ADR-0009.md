# ADR-0009: Knowledge base and code forge are future platform modules

## Status

Accepted — 2026-08-27 (CEO ruling, ecosystem-architecture session). **Draft for 15 months; resolved
against the membership test supplied by ADR-0073.**

## Context

This project must balance remote execution, AI agency, restricted-network deployment, security, mobile UX and future engineering-OS ambitions.

## Decision

Confluence/Jira/GitHub-inspired future is valid but should not enter MVP scope as full clones.

## Consequences

- The decision becomes part of the implementation baseline.
- Changes require a follow-up ADR.
- Related requirements, tests and documentation must stay aligned.

## Resolution — 2026-08-27

**Drafted 2026-05, left unresolved for fifteen months.** It was resolvable only once a test existed
for what "platform module" means; **ADR-0073 §1 supplies it.**

**Accepted as written.** The decision claims only that the Confluence/Jira/GitHub-inspired future is
valid but must not enter MVP scope as full clones. That is true, and the as-built has conformed to it
without exception: REQ-005 (Work Board) and the Knowledge Base module are both NOT-STARTED after
fourteen releases, and no clone of either was attempted.

**What ADR-0073 adds, and it raises the bar rather than lowering it:** a knowledge base or forge that
merely *stores* does not qualify as an ecosystem member. Under ADR-0073 §1 it must record its
consequential actions on the signed ledger in externally verifiable form. That is more engineering
per pillar than a conventional wiki or board costs, and it is the price of the governance claim —
see ADR-0073 §Consequences, where this is recorded as a cost the CEO will not like.

**Not re-litigated here:** whether either module is ever built, and in which release. This ADR says
only *not as an MVP clone*, and that remains its whole content.
